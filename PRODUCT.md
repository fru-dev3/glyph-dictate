# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Mac users who type all day and would rather talk: people filling forms, writing email and
chat, and prompting AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex) in the terminal. Many already pay for
cloud dictation and are uneasy that every word goes to someone's server. They find Jot through
GitHub, fru.dev and search, and decide in under a minute whether to download.

## Product Purpose

Jot is free, open source dictation for the Mac (macOS 26, Apple silicon). Hold Fn, talk, let go,
and the words are typed wherever the cursor is, in any app. Success: a visitor downloads the DMG
(or stars the repo), grants three permissions, and dictates into their own app the same minute.

## Positioning

Everything runs on the Mac: Apple's on-device speech model and Apple Intelligence. No account,
no subscription, no cloud, no audio saved, no network requests of its own. MIT, source on GitHub.
Cloud dictation apps cannot truthfully say any of that.

## Operating Context

Push to talk on the Fn key, a small floating pill at the bottom of the screen (sound bars, time,
word count), a main window (Home with history and stats, Dictionary, Snippets, Styles, Settings),
and a menu bar icon. Distributed as a signed, notarized DMG on GitHub Releases.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Hold Fn to dictate; double-press for hands-free; Fn + Shift saves a thought as Markdown; Fn +
Control is Command Mode; Fn + Option asks your dictation history; "fill the form, ..." fills
every field; "press enter" / "send it" press Return; the mic stays ready with under a second of
pre-roll; cleans "no wait" corrections while you talk; learns spellings from your fixes; terminal
symbols (--no-verify); drafts every 10 s and crash recovery; per-app styles; Dictionary;
Snippets; searchable History. Needs macOS 26 on Apple silicon; Apple Intelligence for clean-up,
Command Mode, Ask and forms.

## Brand Commitments

Made by fru.dev (@fru). Office green #008000 (#3FA34D on dark), never gold. No em dashes, no hype.
Big legible headers, no tiny tracked-uppercase labels. lucide-style icons, never emoji as icons.
The app icon: a white microphone on an office green rounded tile.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: the app itself (screenshots can be taken of it), the icon, the open source repo, the
notarized release, version 0.7.1. No users, testimonials, download counts or benchmarks exist yet:
never invent them.

## Product Principles

- Private by construction: say only what is literally true about where words go.
- One key: the whole product is "hold Fn and talk".
- Show, don't claim: the pill and real words landing in real apps are the proof.
