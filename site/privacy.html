Jot

Privacy

The app

Jot collects nothing. Speech recognition and clean-up run on your Mac with Apple's on-device models. Jot makes no network requests of its own, never writes audio to disk, and keeps your dictations only on your Mac (Application Support and Documents, or a notes folder you choose). There are no accounts and no analytics in the app.

This site

jot.fru.dev counts visits with Google Analytics (pages viewed, rough location, device type), loaded after the page so it never slows it down. In the EU, UK and Switzerland analytics cookies are off by default. The download and GitHub buttons are counted as clicks.
