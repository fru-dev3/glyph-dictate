Everything Jot does

Hold to talk

Hold your talk key, say what you want, and let go. The words are typed wherever your cursor is: email, chat, documents, forms, the terminal, any app.

Hands-free

Double-press the key and keep talking for as long as you like. Press it once more to stop.

Your choice of key

Fn by default, or Right Option, Right Command or Right Control.

Cleans up as you talk

Ums, ahs and false starts are taken out, and long rambles are tidied while you are still talking, so the text lands about a second after you let go.

Corrections

Change your mind mid-sentence. Say "Tuesday, no wait, Thursday" and only Thursday is typed.

Lists

Say "one", "two", "three" as you go and get a numbered list.

A style for each app

Formal in Mail, relaxed in Slack and Messages, exact in the terminal.

Learns your words

Fix a name or a term Jot got wrong, and it spells it right from then on. Add your own words in the Dictionary.

Terminal and code

Say "dash dash", "slash" or "dot" and get the symbols. End with "send it" and Jot presses Return.

Fill a whole form

Say "Fill the form, name Sam Lee, city Austin, email sam at example dot com" and every field is filled. Password fields are left alone.

Rewrite what you selected

Select some text and say what you want instead: "make it shorter", "make it friendlier", "turn it into bullets".

Thoughts

Catch an idea without typing anything. It is saved as a Markdown note in the folder you choose, an Obsidian vault too.

Ask what you said

"When is the launch now?" Jot answers from your own dictations, on your Mac.

Never misses the first word

The mic is always ready, so the word you said as the key went down is kept.

Never loses a ramble

What you say is saved as you talk. If anything goes wrong, it is on your clipboard, and if speech stalls Jot restarts it and hears it all again.

Nowhere to type

No text field in focus? Your words wait on the clipboard, ready to paste.

Private by design

Speech and clean-up run on your Mac with Apple's own models. Nothing to download, no account, no audio saved, and Jot never types into a password field.
